# NovaTech Remote Work Policy

_Effective 1 March 2026. Owner: People Team._

## Hybrid working

Hybrid employees may work remotely up to **3 days per week**. At least 2 days per week must be spent in a NovaTech
office. Team leads may set a fixed "team day" when everyone is in the office.

## Fully remote roles

A fully remote arrangement requires written approval from a **Vice President** of the employee's department.
Fully remote employees must attend the annual company offsite in person.

## Core hours

All employees, remote or not, must be available during core hours of **10:00 to 15:00** in their local time zone.
Outside core hours, working times are flexible.

## Home office support

Employees who work remotely at least 2 days per week receive a **one-time home office stipend of $500** for desks,
chairs and monitors, claimed within 90 days of becoming eligible. They also receive an internet allowance of
**$40 per month**, paid with salary.

## Working abroad

Working from another country is allowed for up to **20 working days per calendar year**, and must be approved by
the employee's manager and the People Team at least 15 working days before travel.
