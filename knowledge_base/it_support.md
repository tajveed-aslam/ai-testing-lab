# NovaTech IT Support

_Effective 1 February 2026. Owner: IT Service Desk._

## Contacting the service desk

The IT Service Desk can be reached by email at **it-help@novatech.example** or by phone on extension **4357**.
The service desk is staffed from 08:00 to 20:00, Monday to Friday.

## Ticket priorities and response times

- **P1 (critical)**, e.g. a company-wide outage or a security incident: first response within **1 hour**, 24/7.
- **P2 (high)**, e.g. one person cannot work at all: first response within **4 hours**.
- **P3 (normal)**, e.g. a software request or a minor fault: first response within **1 business day**.

## Laptops and equipment

Every employee receives a company laptop. Laptops are refreshed every **36 months**. A lost or stolen laptop must be
reported to the service desk within **24 hours** so it can be remotely wiped.

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least **14 characters** long and are changed every **90 days**. Multi-factor authentication is
mandatory for email, VPN and all company systems.

## Software

Only software from the NovaTech Software Center may be installed. Requests for other software are raised as P3
tickets and reviewed by IT Security.
