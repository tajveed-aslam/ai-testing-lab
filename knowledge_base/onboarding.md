# NovaTech Onboarding Guide

_Effective 1 January 2026. Owner: People Team._

## Before your first day

Your laptop is shipped to your home address **5 business days** before your start date. You will receive login
details by email the day before you start.

## Your first day

Orientation starts at **09:30** on your first day, online or at the nearest office. It covers company values, the HR
portal, the expenses tool and IT security basics.

## Your onboarding buddy

Every new joiner is paired with an onboarding buddy from another team for their first **3 months**. Your buddy is your
first contact for "how do things work here" questions.

## 30-60-90 day plan

Within your first week your manager agrees a 30-60-90 day plan with you, with goals for each of your first three
months.

## Probation

New employees have a probation period of **6 months**. A probation review meeting takes place in the final month.
During probation the notice period is 2 weeks; after probation it is 1 month.
