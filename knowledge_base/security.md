# NovaTech Information Security Policy

_Effective 1 February 2026. Owner: IT Security._

## Phishing

Suspicious emails must be reported with the "Report Phish" button within **24 hours** of being received. Never click
links or open attachments in a suspected phishing email.

## Public networks

The NovaTech VPN must be switched on whenever you use public or hotel Wi-Fi.

## Removable media

USB storage devices are **not allowed** on company laptops. Use the company cloud drive to share files.

## Data classification

NovaTech data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Restricted data (for example customer
payment details) may only be stored in systems approved by IT Security and must never be sent by email.

## Clean desk

Laptops must be locked when unattended, and no Confidential or Restricted documents may be left on desks overnight.
