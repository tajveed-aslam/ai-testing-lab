# NovaTech Leave Policy

_Effective 1 January 2026. Owner: People Team._

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive **25 days** of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive annual
leave pro rata to their contracted hours. Leave is accrued monthly at 2.08 days per month.

Annual leave must be requested in the HR portal at least **10 working days** in advance for any absence longer than
3 consecutive days. Shorter absences need 2 working days' notice.

## Carry-over

Up to **5 unused days** may be carried over into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by **31 March**;
any carried-over days not used by then are lost. Carry-over above 5 days is not allowed, even with manager approval.

## Sick leave

Employees receive **10 days** of paid sick leave per year. A doctor's note is required for sickness lasting more than
**3 consecutive days**. Sick leave does not carry over.

## Parental leave

Birth parents receive **16 weeks** of fully paid parental leave. Non-birth parents receive **6 weeks** of fully paid
leave. Parental leave can start up to 2 weeks before the expected due date.

## Observance and pilgrimage leave

Employees may take up to **3 days** of paid observance leave per calendar year for days of faith or cultural
observance that are not already public holidays. These days do not reduce annual leave.

For a pilgrimage, employees may take up to **10 working days** of additional unpaid leave once every **2 years**.
Pilgrimage leave must be requested in the HR portal at least **6 weeks** in advance and can be combined with annual
leave.

## Public holidays

NovaTech observes the public holidays of the country in which the employee is employed. These are in addition to
the 25 days of annual leave.
