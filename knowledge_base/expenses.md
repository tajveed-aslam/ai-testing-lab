# NovaTech Expenses Policy

_Effective 1 January 2026. Owner: Finance Team._

## Submitting expenses

Expenses must be submitted in the expenses tool within **30 days** of the date the cost was incurred. Claims older
than 30 days are rejected unless the Finance Team grants an exception.

## Receipts

An itemised receipt is required for every expense over **$75**. Expenses of $75 or less can be claimed without a
receipt, with a short description of the purchase.

## Approval limits

Expenses up to $500 are approved by the employee's line manager. Any single expense over **$500** also requires
approval from the department's Finance Business Partner.

## Meals and travel

The daily meal allowance is **$60 per day** for domestic travel and **$90 per day** for international travel.
Alcohol is never reimbursed. Economy class must be used for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is allowed for
flights of 6 hours or longer. Personal car use is reimbursed at **$0.58 per mile**.

## Payment

Approved expenses are paid with the next monthly salary, provided they are approved by the **20th of the month**.
